# Chapter 1: Getting Started

> Everything you need to go from "I just got my login" to "I know my way around."

---

## Your First Login

### What you'll see:

When you open CivitasOne for the first time in your browser, you'll land on a sign-in page with the CivitasOne logo, a language chooser at the top right, and two fields: your email address and password.

`[Screenshot: Sign-in page with email and password fields]`

### Steps to sign in:

1. Type your official email address in the **Email** field.
2. Type the temporary password you received (check your email from your IT admin).
3. Tap the blue **Sign in** button.
4. If your office uses two-step verification (MFA), you'll be asked for a code from your authenticator app. Enter it and tap **Verify**.
5. On your very first login, the system asks you to set a new password. Pick something you'll remember — at least 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers.
6. Tap **Set password**. You're in!

> If you forget your password later, tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. A reset link goes to your email.

## The Welcome Tour (4 Steps)

### What you'll see:

Right after your first sign-in, a friendly overlay appears — a short guided tour that highlights the main parts of the screen. It has four steps, and you can skip it if you like (there's a "Skip tour" link at the bottom).

`[Screenshot: Welcome tour overlay highlighting the sidebar]`

### The four tour stops:

1. **The sidebar** — This is your menu. Every module you have access to (like Finance, HR, Procurement) appears here. Tap any item to open it.
2. **The top bar** — Shows your name, your office, notifications (the bell icon), and the language switcher.
3. **Quick actions** — The "+" button at the top right lets you jump straight to common tasks like "Create bill" or "Apply leave" without hunting through menus.
4. **Help & AI assistant** — The "?" icon opens help tips, and the **Ask CivitasOne** button opens an AI chat that answers questions about the module you're looking at.

When you finish the tour, tap **Done**. You won't see it again, but you can always replay it from **Help Centre → Replay Tour**.

## The Setup Wizard (8 Steps)

### What you'll see:

If you are the first person in your office to sign in (the "owner" or admin), you'll see the **Setup Wizard** — a step-by-step guide to get your office ready. There's a progress bar at the top showing how far you are. You can pause and come back any time; your progress is saved.

`[Screenshot: Setup Wizard with progress bar showing Step 1 of 8]`

> Only the office admin sees the setup wizard. If you're a regular user, your admin has already done this — you can skip to the next section.

### Step 1: Tell us about your office

**What you'll see:** A form asking for your office name, address, phone number, and type of organisation (Government Department, PSU, or Small Office).

1. Fill in your office's official name.
2. Add the full address, district, state, and PIN code.
3. Pick the organisation type from the dropdown.
4. Tap **Save & continue**.

### Step 2: Add your branch offices

**What you'll see:** A list (empty at first) with an **Add branch** button. Your head office appears automatically from Step 1.

1. Tap **Add branch**.
2. Type the branch name and address.
3. Pick which office it reports to (its "parent").
4. Repeat for each branch. When done, tap **Continue**.

> If your office has no branches, just tap **Continue** — you can always add them later.

### Step 3: Set up departments

**What you'll see:** A form to create teams/sections within your office (like "Accounts", "Administration", "Engineering").

1. Tap **Add department**.
2. Type the department name.
3. Optionally pick the branch it belongs to.
4. Repeat until done, then tap **Continue**.

### Step 4: Invite your team

**What you'll see:** A people list with an **Invite** button. Each row shows name, email, and role.

1. Tap **Invite people**.
2. Type the person's name and official email.
3. Choose a role — this decides what they can see and do. (Common roles: Officer, Clerk, Approver, View-only.)
4. Tap **Send invite**. They'll get an email with a link to create their account.
5. Repeat for everyone. Tap **Continue** when done.

### Step 5: Choose the parts you use

**What you'll see:** A grid of module cards (Finance, HR, Procurement, Projects, etc.) with toggle switches.

1. Turn **on** the modules your office actually uses.
2. Leave the rest off — you can turn them on later from **Admin → Settings**.
3. Tap **Continue**.

### Step 6: Set your financial year and accounts

**What you'll see:** A dropdown for the financial year (e.g. 2025–26) and a section to set up your account heads (Head of Account / HoA).

1. Pick the current financial year from the dropdown.
2. The system loads the standard government Chart of Accounts. Review it — you can add sub-heads later.
3. Tap **Continue**.

### Step 7: Set up leave rules

**What you'll see:** A table of leave types (Casual Leave, Earned Leave, etc.) with a column for "days per year."

1. Check that the default leave types match your office rules.
2. Adjust the number of days if needed.
3. Add any special leave types your office uses (e.g. Special Casual Leave).
4. Tap **Continue**.

### Step 8: Set up pay structure

**What you'll see:** A form listing salary components — Basic Pay, DA, HRA, and deductions like GPF, NPS, Professional Tax.

1. Review the default pay heads. Most government offices can use them as-is.
2. Add or rename components if your office has different allowances.
3. Tap **Finish setup**.

You'll see a green confirmation: **"Setup complete!"** — and you'll be taken to your dashboard.

## Choosing Your Language

### What you'll see:

At the top right of every page, there's a globe icon or the word **Language**. Tap it to see the available languages.

`[Screenshot: Language dropdown showing English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada]`

### Supported languages:

| Language | Label shown |
|----------|------------|
| English | English |
| Hindi | हिन्दी |
| Tamil | தமிழ் |
| Telugu | తెలుగు |
| Kannada | ಕನ್ನಡ |

### To switch language:

1. Tap the **Language** dropdown in the top bar.
2. Choose your preferred language.
3. The entire interface changes instantly — menus, buttons, labels, everything.

> Your choice is saved to your account. Next time you sign in, the system remembers.

## How to Get Help

CivitasOne gives you four ways to find answers without calling IT:

### 1. Tooltip help (the "?" icons)

Wherever you see a small **?** icon next to a label, hover over it (or tap it on mobile). A short explanation appears telling you what that field or term means.

### 2. Help Centre

Tap the **?** button in the bottom-left corner (or go to your profile menu → **Help Centre**). You'll find:
- Guides for each module
- Step-by-step walkthroughs
- Answers to common questions

### 3. AI Assistant (Ask CivitasOne)

Tap the **Ask** button (floating at the bottom right). A chat window opens. Type your question in plain language — for example, "How do I apply for leave?" — and the assistant will answer based on the module you're viewing.

`[Screenshot: AI assistant chat window with a sample question]`

> The assistant works best for "how do I…" questions. For policy decisions, talk to your supervisor.

### 4. Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** on your keyboard (on desktop) to see a list of keyboard shortcuts. Common ones:

| Shortcut | What it does |
|----------|-------------|
| `Ctrl + K` | Open quick search — find anything fast |
| `Ctrl + /` | Open the AI assistant |
| `Ctrl + N` | Create new (whatever you're looking at) |
| `Esc` | Close the current dialog or panel |

## Common Questions

**Q: I can't see a module in my sidebar (like Finance or HR).**
A: Your admin may not have turned it on yet, or your role doesn't include access to it. Ask your admin to check under **Admin → Settings → Modules**.

**Q: I forgot my password.**
A: On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot password?** and follow the email link to reset it.

**Q: Can I use CivitasOne on my phone?**
A: Yes! There's a mobile app — see Chapter 11. You can also open the web version in your phone's browser.

**Q: The screen looks different from what my colleague sees.**
A: Screens change based on your role and which modules are turned on. An officer sees more options than a clerk.

**Q: How do I change my name or email?**
A: Tap your name in the top right → **My profile**. You can update your name and photo. To change your email, ask your admin.
