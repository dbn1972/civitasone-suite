# Chapter 3: HR & Payroll

> Manage your people — joining, attendance, leave, transfers, salaries, and yearly reviews.

---

## Employee Directory

### What you'll see:

Open **HR** from the sidebar. The first screen shows the **Employee Directory** — a searchable list of everyone in your office. Each row shows the person's name, photo (if uploaded), designation, department, and status (Active, On Leave, Deputation, Retired).

`[Screenshot: Employee directory with search bar and department filter]`

### Search for someone

1. Type a name, employee ID, or designation in the **Search** bar at the top.
2. Results filter as you type.
3. Use the **Department** dropdown to narrow to one team.
4. Tap a person's row to open their full profile.

### View an employee profile

1. Open the person from the directory.
2. You'll see tabs: **Personal**, **Service**, **Pay**, **Leave**, **Documents**.
3. **Personal** — name, date of birth, contact, emergency contacts.
4. **Service** — joining date, current posting, promotion history, transfer history.
5. **Pay** — current pay level, allowances, bank account.
6. **Leave** — balances for each leave type.
7. **Documents** — uploaded ID proofs, appointment orders, etc.

> You can only see the profiles your role allows. A clerk sees basic info; an HR officer sees everything.

## Leave Management

### What you'll see:

Open **HR → Leave**. You'll see two views depending on your role:
- **As an employee:** Your own leave balance, a calendar showing your leave days, and a button to apply.
- **As an approver:** A list of pending leave requests from your team.

`[Screenshot: Leave dashboard with balance cards and calendar view]`

### Apply for leave

1. Tap **+ Apply Leave**.
2. Choose the **Leave Type** from the dropdown (Casual Leave, Earned Leave, Half Pay Leave, etc.).
3. Pick the **From Date** and **To Date** using the calendar.
4. If it's a half-day, tick the **Half Day** checkbox and pick Morning or Afternoon.
5. Type a short **Reason**.
6. Tap **Submit**. Your request goes to your reporting officer.
7. You'll see a confirmation: "Leave request submitted."

### Approve or reject leave (for approvers)

1. Open **HR → Leave → Pending Approvals**.
2. Tap the request to view the details — who, what type, how many days, and reason.
3. Check the person's leave balance shown on the side.
4. Tap **Approve** or **Reject**. If rejecting, you must add a reason.
5. The employee gets a notification of your decision.

### Check your leave balance

1. Open **HR → Leave**.
2. Your balance cards appear at the top: one card per leave type (e.g. CL: 8 remaining, EL: 15 remaining).
3. Tap any card to see the breakdown — how many were earned, used, and lapsed.

## Attendance

### What you'll see:

Open **HR → Attendance**. A monthly calendar view shows each day colour-coded: green (present), red (absent), blue (leave), grey (holiday/weekend).

`[Screenshot: Attendance calendar with colour-coded days]`

### GPS check-in (for field or mobile users)

1. Open the CivitasOne mobile app.
2. Tap **Check In** on the home screen.
3. Allow location access when asked. Your GPS location is captured.
4. Take a selfie when prompted (this confirms it's you).
5. Tap **Submit**. You'll see: "Checked in at [time]."
6. At the end of your day, repeat the process with **Check Out**.

> Your office may have a "geo-fence" — a boundary around the office. If you check in from too far away, you'll see a warning and may need to apply for regularisation.

### Mark attendance regularisation

If you forgot to check in, or the system marked you absent by mistake:

1. Open **HR → Attendance → Regularisation**.
2. Tap **+ Request Regularisation**.
3. Pick the **Date** that needs correcting.
4. Choose the correct status: **Present**, **Work from Home**, **On Duty (field)**.
5. Add a short **Reason** (e.g. "Biometric not working" or "Was on field duty").
6. Tap **Submit**. Your supervisor approves or rejects it.

## Payroll

### What you'll see:

Open **HR → Payroll**. The main screen shows payroll runs — each row is a month, showing the status (Draft, Processing, Approved, Paid) and the total amount.

`[Screenshot: Payroll runs list with monthly rows and status badges]`

### Run monthly payroll

1. Tap **+ New Payroll Run**.
2. Pick the **Month and Year** (e.g. June 2025).
3. The system calculates salaries for all active employees based on their pay structure, attendance, and leave (LOP days reduce the salary automatically).
4. You'll see a summary: total gross, total deductions, and net payable.
5. Review the list — tap any employee's name to see their individual breakdown.
6. If everything looks correct, tap **Submit for Approval**.
7. An authorised officer reviews and taps **Approve**.

### Generate pay slips

1. Open the approved payroll run.
2. Tap **Generate Payslips**.
3. The system creates a PDF slip for each employee.
4. To view one: tap the employee's name → **View Payslip**.
5. To send all: tap **Email Payslips** — each person gets theirs by email.

### Generate bank file

1. Open the approved payroll run.
2. Tap **Generate Bank File**.
3. Choose your bank's format (NEFT, custom format, etc.).
4. Tap **Download**. You'll get a file to upload to your bank's portal for salary transfer.

> The bank file includes each employee's account number, IFSC, and net salary. Double-check bank details are up to date before generating.

## Recruitment

### What you'll see:

Open **HR → Recruitment**. A board view shows open vacancies as cards, with columns for each stage: **Draft**, **Published**, **Applications**, **Shortlisted**, **Interviewed**, **Offered**, **Joined**.

`[Screenshot: Recruitment board with vacancy cards moving through stages]`

### Post a vacancy

1. Tap **+ New Vacancy**.
2. Enter the **Post Title** (e.g. "Junior Clerk"), **Department**, and **Number of Posts**.
3. Add the **Qualifications** and **Experience** required.
4. Set the **Last Date** to apply.
5. Tap **Publish**. The vacancy appears on the public careers page (if enabled) and moves to the Published column.

### Receive and review applications

1. Open the vacancy card.
2. Tap the **Applications** tab. Each application shows the candidate's name, qualifications, and attached resume.
3. Tap an application to read the full details.

### Shortlist candidates

1. In the application list, tick the candidates you want to move forward.
2. Tap **Move to Shortlist**.
3. Optionally send them a notification (email or SMS) about the next step.

## Training

### What you'll see:

Open **HR → Training**. A list of training programmes — each showing title, dates, trainer, and number of nominees.

`[Screenshot: Training list with upcoming and past programmes]`

### Schedule a training programme

1. Tap **+ New Training**.
2. Enter the **Title**, **Description**, **Start Date**, **End Date**, and **Venue** (or "Online").
3. Add the **Trainer** name and contact.
4. Set the **Maximum Participants** if there's a limit.
5. Tap **Save**.

### Nominate employees

1. Open the training programme.
2. Tap **+ Nominate**.
3. Search for and select the employees you want to nominate.
4. Tap **Confirm**. They'll get a notification about the training.

### Track completion

1. Open the training programme after it's done.
2. Tap **Mark Attendance**.
3. Tick who attended and who didn't.
4. Tap **Save**. Their training records update automatically.

## APAR (Annual Performance Appraisal)

### What you'll see:

Open **HR → APAR**. A list shows all appraisal records for the current year. Each row shows the employee name, appraisal year, and current stage (Self-Assessment, Reporting Officer, Reviewing Officer, Accepting Authority).

`[Screenshot: APAR list with stage indicators]`

### Initiate an APAR

1. Tap **+ Initiate APAR**.
2. Pick the **Appraisal Year** and the **Employee**.
3. The system creates a blank APAR form and sends it to the employee for self-assessment.

### Fill the self-assessment (as an employee)

1. Open your APAR from the notification or from **HR → APAR → My Appraisals**.
2. Fill in your **KRAs** (Key Result Areas) and how you performed against each.
3. Add any training attended, special achievements, or difficulties.
4. Tap **Submit to Reporting Officer**.

### Review and grade (as reporting/reviewing officer)

1. Open the APAR from **HR → APAR → Pending Reviews**.
2. Read the employee's self-assessment.
3. Enter your assessment and the **Overall Grading**.
4. Tap **Submit to Reviewing Officer** (or **Submit to Accepting Authority** if you're the reviewer).

### Accept (as accepting authority)

1. Open the APAR from your pending list.
2. Review all assessments.
3. Tap **Accept** to finalise, or **Return** with remarks if changes are needed.
4. Once accepted, the APAR is locked and becomes part of the service record.

## Transfer & Promotion

### What you'll see:

Open **HR → Transfer/Promotion**. A list of orders — each showing the employee, type (Transfer/Promotion), from/to posting, and status.

`[Screenshot: Transfer orders list with status badges]`

### Initiate a transfer

1. Tap **+ New Transfer**.
2. Search for and select the **Employee**.
3. Enter the **From** posting (auto-filled from their current posting) and **To** posting.
4. Pick the **Effective Date**.
5. Attach the transfer order document (if available).
6. Tap **Submit for Approval**.
7. The request follows your office's approval chain (typically goes through an eOffice file for higher posts).

### Initiate a promotion

1. Tap **+ New Promotion**.
2. Select the **Employee**.
3. Enter the new **Designation** and **Pay Level**.
4. Pick the **Effective Date**.
5. Attach the promotion order.
6. Tap **Submit for Approval**.

> For transfers and promotions that need eOffice routing, the system automatically creates a file and attaches the order as a note. You'll see a link to the eOffice file in the record.

## Common Questions

**Q: I applied for leave but it still shows "Pending." How long does approval take?**
A: It depends on your reporting officer. If it's been more than 2 days, you can send a reminder from the leave request screen (tap **Send Reminder**).

**Q: My attendance shows "Absent" but I was working from home.**
A: Apply for a Regularisation (HR → Attendance → Regularisation) and select "Work from Home" as the reason.

**Q: Payroll shows the wrong salary for an employee.**
A: Check their pay structure (HR → employee profile → Pay tab). If an allowance changed, update it there before re-running payroll for that month.

**Q: Can I run payroll for just one person?**
A: Yes — when creating a payroll run, use the **Filter** to select a single employee or department.

**Q: The APAR form is stuck at "Reporting Officer" stage.**
A: The reporting officer hasn't submitted their assessment yet. Send them a reminder or contact HR admin to re-assign if needed.

**Q: How do I see an employee's complete service history?**
A: Open their profile → **Service** tab. It shows every posting, transfer, and promotion since joining.
