# Chapter 5: Projects & Grants

> Plan and track projects — phases, tasks, milestones, and spending. Disburse grant money and track how it's used.

---

## Projects

### What you'll see:

Open **Projects** from the sidebar. The main screen shows a list (or card view) of all projects. Each project card shows the name, budget, percentage complete, and a status badge (Planning, In Progress, Delayed, Completed, On Hold).

`[Screenshot: Projects list with progress bars and status badges]`

### Create a new project

1. Tap **+ New Project**.
2. Enter the **Project Name** and a short **Description**.
3. Set the **Start Date** and **Expected End Date**.
4. Enter the **Total Budget** (in rupees).
5. Choose the **Head of Account** or funding source.
6. Assign a **Project Manager** (search by name).
7. Pick the **Department** or branch responsible.
8. Tap **Create**. The project appears in your list with status "Planning."

### Add milestones

1. Open the project.
2. Tap the **Milestones** tab.
3. Tap **+ Add Milestone**.
4. Enter the **Milestone Name** (e.g. "Foundation complete", "Handover to department").
5. Set the **Target Date**.
6. Optionally enter the **Budget Allocated** to this milestone.
7. Tap **Save**. Repeat for each major checkpoint.

> Milestones are the big checkpoints. Use tasks (below) for the smaller pieces of work within each milestone.

### Break work into phases and tasks

1. Open the project → **Work Breakdown** tab.
2. Tap **+ Add Phase** (e.g. "Phase 1: Design", "Phase 2: Construction").
3. Inside each phase, tap **+ Add Task**.
4. For each task, enter:
   - **Task Name**
   - **Assigned To** (person responsible)
   - **Start Date** and **Due Date**
   - **Status** (Not Started, In Progress, Complete)
5. Tap **Save**. Tasks appear nested under their phase.

### Track progress

1. Open the project.
2. The **Overview** tab shows:
   - **Overall progress** — a percentage bar calculated from completed tasks.
   - **Budget utilisation** — how much of the budget is spent vs. remaining.
   - **Timeline** — whether you're on track, ahead, or behind schedule.
3. Update task statuses as work progresses — the overall progress updates automatically.

### Gantt view

1. Open the project → **Gantt** tab.
2. You'll see a horizontal timeline chart. Each phase and task is a bar showing its start-to-end span.
3. Bars are colour-coded: green (on track), amber (at risk), red (delayed).
4. Drag a bar's end to change a due date (if you have permission).
5. Dependencies between tasks show as arrows connecting the bars.

`[Screenshot: Gantt chart showing phases, tasks, and dependency arrows]`

> The Gantt view is great for spotting bottlenecks — look for red bars or chains of tasks that depend on one delayed task.

## Grants

### What you'll see:

Open **Grants** from the sidebar. A list of all grants your office manages — showing the scheme name, grantee, total sanctioned amount, amount disbursed so far, and UC status.

`[Screenshot: Grants list with disbursement progress and UC status]`

### Register a grantee

1. Open **Grants → Grantees**.
2. Tap **+ Add Grantee**.
3. Enter the grantee's details:
   - **Name** (organisation or individual)
   - **Type** (NGO, Panchayat, Self-Help Group, Individual, etc.)
   - **Address** and **Contact Person**
   - **Bank Account** details (for DBT — Direct Benefit Transfer)
   - **Aadhaar / PAN** (if individual)
4. Attach registration documents (society registration, etc.).
5. Tap **Save**. The grantee is now available for grant disbursements.

### Disburse an installment

1. Open the grant record.
2. Tap the **Disbursements** tab.
3. Tap **+ Release Installment**.
4. Pick the **Installment Number** (1st, 2nd, 3rd…).
5. Enter the **Amount** to release.
6. Check that the release conditions are met (shown on screen — e.g. "Previous UC submitted" or "Milestone achieved").
7. Tap **Submit for Approval**.
8. Once the approving officer approves, the payment is initiated to the grantee's bank account.
9. You'll see the disbursement status change to **Released** once the payment goes through.

### Submit a Utilisation Certificate (UC)

1. Open the grant record → **Utilisation Certificates** tab.
2. Tap **+ Submit UC**.
3. Enter the **Period** the UC covers.
4. Fill in the **Expenditure Statement** — how the grant money was spent, line by line.
5. Enter any **Unspent Balance** (money not yet used).
6. Attach supporting documents (receipts, completion certificates, photos of work done).
7. Tap **Submit**.
8. The reviewing officer checks and either **Accepts** or **Returns** the UC with remarks.

### Close a grant

1. Open the grant record.
2. Ensure:
   - All installments are disbursed (or remaining ones are cancelled with reason).
   - All UCs are submitted and accepted.
   - Any unspent balance is returned (refund recorded).
3. Tap **Close Grant**.
4. Add closing remarks.
5. Tap **Confirm**. The grant status changes to **Closed** and no further disbursements can be made.

## Common Questions

**Q: A project's progress percentage seems wrong.**
A: The percentage is calculated from tasks. If you haven't broken work into tasks, progress will show as 0%. Add tasks and update their statuses.

**Q: Can I change a project's budget after it's created?**
A: Yes — open the project → **Budget** tab → **Revise Budget**. Enter the new amount with a reason. This is recorded in the audit trail.

**Q: The Gantt chart is too wide to read.**
A: Use the zoom controls at the top right of the Gantt view. You can zoom to Day, Week, Month, or Quarter view.

**Q: A grant installment is stuck at "Pending Approval."**
A: Check the release conditions — the system won't let the approver proceed if conditions aren't met (like a previous UC being overdue). Resolve the condition first.

**Q: The grantee says they didn't receive the money.**
A: Open the disbursement record and check the **Payment Status** and **UTR**. If the payment was processed, share the UTR with the grantee's bank for tracing. If it failed, you'll see an error message — re-initiate the payment.

**Q: Can I track physical progress (like construction) in the project?**
A: Yes — use milestones for physical progress markers. You can also attach photos to tasks as proof of completion.
