# Chapter 6: Establishment

> Run the office's day-to-day paperwork — files and notes, meetings, vehicles, and RTI responses.

---

## eOffice Files

### What you'll see:

Open **Establishment → Files**. You'll see your desk — a list of files currently with you, divided into **Inbox** (files sent to you for action), **Sent** (files you've forwarded), and **Created by Me**. Each row shows the file number, subject, who sent it, and when.

`[Screenshot: Files inbox showing pending files with subject and sender]`

### Create a new file

1. Tap **+ Create File**.
2. Enter the **Subject** — a clear title for the matter (e.g. "Approval for annual maintenance contract").
3. Choose the **File Category** (General, Confidential, Secret, etc.).
4. Pick the **Department** the file belongs to.
5. Tap **Create**. The file gets a unique file number automatically.
6. You'll land on the file's **Note Sheet** — this is where all notes and decisions are recorded.

### Add a note to a file

1. Open the file.
2. Tap the **Note Sheet** tab (it should already be open).
3. Tap **+ Add Note**.
4. Type your note — explain the matter, give your recommendation, or record a decision.
5. You can format text with bold, bullets, and numbered lists.
6. Attach any documents (letters, reports, quotes) using the **Attach** button.
7. Tap **Save Note**. Your note appears on the note sheet with your name, designation, and the date/time.

### Forward a file

1. Open the file.
2. Tap **Forward** at the top.
3. Search for the person or desk you're sending it to.
4. Add a brief forwarding remark (e.g. "For approval please" or "For information and necessary action").
5. Tap **Send**. The file moves to their inbox.
6. Your copy shows in the **Sent** tab.

### Make a decision on a file

1. Open the file from your inbox.
2. Read the notes from previous desks.
3. Add your own note with the decision (Approved / Rejected / More Information Needed).
4. If approved, tap **Forward** to send it to the next step (or back to the originator).
5. If the matter is concluded, tap **Close File**. The file is archived but still searchable.

> Files in CivitasOne follow the same flow as physical files in eOffice. Notes build up in sequence, and every action is recorded with who did what and when.

## Meetings

### What you'll see:

Open **Establishment → Meetings**. A calendar and list view of all meetings — upcoming ones at the top. Each entry shows the title, date/time, venue, and chairperson.

`[Screenshot: Meetings list with upcoming meetings highlighted]`

### Schedule a meeting

1. Tap **+ New Meeting**.
2. Enter the **Title** (e.g. "Monthly Review Meeting").
3. Pick the **Date**, **Start Time**, and **End Time**.
4. Enter the **Venue** (room name, address, or "Virtual — link will be shared").
5. Choose the **Chairperson** from the people list.
6. Add **Attendees** — search and select each person.
7. Add the **Agenda** — a numbered list of topics to discuss.
8. Tap **Send Invites**. All attendees get a notification and email.

### Record Minutes of Meeting (MoM)

1. After the meeting, open the meeting record.
2. Tap **+ Record Minutes**.
3. For each agenda item, type:
   - **Discussion** — a summary of what was discussed.
   - **Decision** — what was decided.
   - **Action Items** — tasks assigned, with the person responsible and deadline.
4. Tap **Save Minutes**.
5. The MoM is saved and can be shared with all attendees.

### Assign action items from a meeting

1. Open the meeting's MoM.
2. For each action item, the system creates a trackable task.
3. The assigned person sees the action item in their **Tasks** list.
4. As they complete it, they mark it done.
5. The chairperson can check progress from the meeting record — each action item shows "Pending" or "Done."

## Vehicles

### What you'll see:

Open **Establishment → Vehicles**. A list of all office vehicles: registration number, make/model, assigned driver, and current status (Available, In Use, Under Maintenance).

`[Screenshot: Vehicle list with status badges and mileage]`

### Log a trip

1. Open the vehicle's record.
2. Tap **+ Log Trip**.
3. Enter:
   - **Date** and **Purpose** (e.g. "Official visit to District HQ").
   - **Officer Name** (who used the vehicle).
   - **Starting KM** and **Ending KM** (odometer readings).
   - **Fuel Added** (litres and cost, if any).
4. Tap **Save**. The log is recorded and the vehicle's total mileage updates.

### Record maintenance

1. Open the vehicle's record.
2. Tap **+ Maintenance Entry**.
3. Enter the **Type** (servicing, tyre change, repair, etc.).
4. Enter the **Date**, **Vendor/Garage**, and **Cost**.
5. Add a brief description of work done.
6. Attach the bill if available.
7. Tap **Save**. The vehicle's maintenance history is updated.

### View reports

1. Open **Establishment → Vehicles → Reports**.
2. Choose:
   - **Trip Log Report** — all trips for a date range.
   - **Fuel Report** — fuel consumption per vehicle.
   - **Maintenance Report** — all repairs and costs.
   - **Utilisation Report** — how often each vehicle is used vs. sitting idle.
3. Pick the date range and tap **Generate**. Download as PDF or Excel.

## RTI (Right to Information)

### What you'll see:

Open **Establishment → RTI**. A list of RTI requests received by your office: application number, applicant name, date received, subject, deadline (30 days from receipt), and status (Received, Assigned, Response Sent, Appeal, Closed).

`[Screenshot: RTI list with countdown timers showing days remaining]`

### Receive and register an RTI request

1. Tap **+ Register RTI**.
2. Enter the applicant's **Name**, **Address**, and **Contact**.
3. Enter the **Date Received** and **Mode** (online, post, in person).
4. Type or paste the **Information Requested** — what the applicant is asking for.
5. Enter the **Fee Paid** (if applicable).
6. Tap **Save**. The system assigns a number and starts the 30-day clock.

### Assign to the responsible section

1. Open the RTI request.
2. Tap **Assign**.
3. Search for the **Section/Person** who holds the information.
4. Add a note if needed (e.g. "Please provide the relevant records by [date]").
5. Tap **Send**. The assigned person sees it in their task list.

### Respond to an RTI request

1. Open the RTI request.
2. Tap **+ Prepare Response**.
3. Type the response — provide the information requested, or explain why it can't be provided (with the relevant section of the RTI Act).
4. Attach any documents the applicant asked for.
5. Tap **Submit for PIO Approval** (the Public Information Officer reviews and signs off).
6. Once approved, tap **Send Response**. The applicant is notified.
7. Status changes to **Response Sent**.

### Handle a first appeal

1. If the applicant files a first appeal, it appears in **RTI → Appeals**.
2. Open the appeal.
3. The First Appellate Authority reviews the original response.
4. Record the decision: **Upheld** (original response stands) or **Modified** (provide additional information).
5. Tap **Save Decision**. The appellant is notified.

> The system tracks the 30-day and 45-day deadlines. When a deadline is approaching, you'll see an amber warning. If it passes, the entry turns red.

## Common Questions

**Q: I forwarded a file to the wrong person. Can I recall it?**
A: If the other person hasn't opened it yet, open the file from your **Sent** tab and tap **Recall**. If they've already opened it, you'll need to ask them to forward it back.

**Q: Where can I see all files I've ever worked on?**
A: Go to **Files → All My Files**. This shows every file that has passed through your desk, including closed ones.

**Q: The meeting MoM needs a correction after it was saved.**
A: Open the MoM and tap **Edit**. Make the change — the system notes that the MoM was amended, with the date of amendment.

**Q: A vehicle is due for servicing. Does the system remind me?**
A: Yes — if you set the **Next Service Due** date or KM in the vehicle record, the system sends a notification when it's approaching.

**Q: The RTI deadline is tomorrow and I haven't responded yet. What happens?**
A: The system marks the request as **Overdue** (red). Your office's compliance report will reflect it. Respond as soon as possible — delayed responses may attract scrutiny from the Information Commission.

**Q: Can I search for old files by subject?**
A: Yes — use the **Search** bar in the Files section. You can search by file number, subject, note content, or attached document names.
