# Chapter 12: Glossary

> Every specialist term in CivitasOne, explained in one plain sentence. If you see a word you don't recognise, look it up here.

---

## A

**Advance** — Money paid out before work or a bill is complete, to be adjusted later with a Utilisation Certificate.

**APAR** — Annual Performance Appraisal Report — the yearly assessment of a government officer's work and behaviour.

**API** — Application Programming Interface — the way CivitasOne connects to other computer systems (you won't use this directly).

**Approving Officer** — The person who checks and authorises a submission (bill, leave, sanction, etc.) before it goes through.

**Asset Tag** — A barcode sticker placed on a physical item (desk, computer, printer) so the system can identify it.

**Audit Trail** — A complete record of who did what and when — the system keeps this automatically for every action.

## B

**Balance Sheet** — A report showing what your office owns (assets) and what it owes (liabilities) at a point in time.

**Bank Guarantee** — A bank's promise to pay if a supplier fails to meet their commitment under a contract.

**BE (Budget Estimate)** — The money planned for the year before any revision.

**Beneficiary** — The person or group who receives the benefit of a scheme or grant.

**Bill** — A document from a vendor requesting payment for goods or services delivered.

**Biometric Lock** — Using your fingerprint or face to unlock the app instead of typing a password.

**Break-glass** — Emergency access granted for a short time, fully recorded, when something urgent happens and normal channels are too slow.

**Budget** — The money set aside for a purpose in a financial year, allocated under a Head of Account.

## C

**Cash Flow** — A report showing money coming in (receipts) and going out (payments) over a period.

**CGST** — Central Goods and Services Tax — the central government's share of GST on a transaction within the same state.

**Challan** — A deposit slip used to pay money into the government treasury or bank.

**Chart of Accounts** — The complete list of account heads under which money is recorded.

**CPC** — Central Pay Commission — the body that recommends government pay scales (currently 7th CPC).

**Cost Centre** — A section or unit whose spending is tracked separately for management purposes.

**Credit Note** — A document reducing the amount owed — issued when goods are returned or an invoice was too high.

**Custodian** — The person officially responsible for looking after an asset.

## D

**DAK** — Incoming and outgoing post or correspondence handled by the office.

**DBT** — Direct Benefit Transfer — scheme money sent straight to the beneficiary's bank account.

**DDO** — Drawing and Disbursing Officer — the official who draws and pays out government money.

**DDO Code** — The official code that identifies a Drawing and Disbursing Officer.

**Debit** — An entry that increases an asset or expense account (one side of a journal entry).

**Department** — A team or section within your office (like Accounts, Administration, or Engineering).

**Depreciation** — The yearly reduction in an asset's value as it ages or wears out.

**Deputation** — Temporarily sending an employee to work in another office or organisation.

**Disbursement** — Releasing money to the person or office that should receive it.

**DPR** — Detailed Project Report — the full plan and cost estimate for a project before it begins.

## E

**EMD** — Earnest Money Deposit — a security deposit paid with a bid to show the bidder is serious.

**eOffice** — The paperless file system for moving notes and approvals between desks, replacing physical file movement.

**ESI** — Employees' State Insurance — a health and benefit scheme for eligible employees.

## F

**Feature Flag** — An on/off switch that controls whether a new feature is visible to users, without needing code changes.

**Financial Year** — The 12-month period used for accounting, running from 1st April to 31st March in India.

**Form 16A** — A certificate showing tax that was deducted from a payment, given to the payee.

## G

**Gantt Chart** — A bar chart showing project tasks over time, with each task as a horizontal bar from start to end date.

**Geo-fence** — An invisible boundary around a location (like your office) used to verify that attendance check-ins happen nearby.

**GL (General Ledger)** — The master record of every money transaction, kept in balance with matching debits and credits.

**GPF** — General Provident Fund — a savings fund where government employees put away money each month, with interest.

**Grantee** — The person or organisation that receives a grant.

**Gratuity** — A one-time payment to an employee on retirement or leaving service after a qualifying period.

**Grievance** — A complaint from a citizen that the office must look into and resolve.

**GRN** — Goods Received Note — the check done when a delivery arrives, recording what was actually received.

**GST** — Goods and Services Tax — the indirect tax on supply of goods and services in India.

**GSTIN** — GST Identification Number — the 15-digit number that identifies a business registered under GST.

## H

**Head of Account (HoA)** — The classification code under which government money is budgeted and spent.

**Helpdesk** — An internal support system where staff raise tickets for IT, facility, or process issues.

## I

**IFSC** — Indian Financial System Code — the code that identifies a bank branch for electronic transfers.

**IGST** — Integrated GST — the tax applied on transactions between different states.

**Indent** — A request to buy goods or services, raised before a purchase order is created.

**Installment** — One part of a grant or payment released in stages rather than all at once.

**IRN** — Invoice Reference Number — the unique ID a tax invoice gets when registered with the government GST portal.

## J

**Journal Entry** — A single accounting record with matching debit and credit amounts, posted to the General Ledger.

## K

**Kill Switch** — An emergency feature flag setting that instantly turns off a feature for all users.

**KRA** — Key Result Area — the main thing a person is expected to deliver in their role, used in APAR.

## L

**LGD Code** — Local Government Directory code — the official ID for a state, district, block, or village.

**LOP** — Loss of Pay — a day with no salary, usually for unapproved absence.

## M

**Maker-Checker** — A control where one person submits and a different person approves, to prevent mistakes and fraud.

**MFA** — Multi-Factor Authentication — a second check (like an app code or OTP) when signing in, for extra security.

**Milestone** — A key checkpoint in a project that must be completed on time.

**Module** — A part of the system you can turn on or off, like Finance, HR, or Procurement.

**MOM** — Minutes of Meeting — the official written record of what a meeting discussed and decided.

## N

**Narration** — A short description written on a journal entry explaining what the transaction is for.

**NEFT** — National Electronic Funds Transfer — a common way to move money between banks.

**NFC** — Near Field Communication — wireless technology for sharing data by tapping two devices together.

**NPS** — National Pension System — a retirement savings scheme for government employees.

**Note Sheet** — The running notes and approvals attached to an official file in eOffice.

## O

**Outbox** — A queue of messages waiting to be sent to other systems (you won't see this, but it keeps things reliable).

## P

**Pay Matrix** — The 7th Pay Commission table that sets salary by grade and level for government employees.

**PFMS** — Public Financial Management System — the government's central payment platform for tracking expenditure.

**PIO** — Public Information Officer — the designated person who responds to RTI requests.

**PKCE** — A secure login method used by the mobile app that keeps your account safe without storing your password on the phone.

**PO (Purchase Order)** — The official order sent to a supplier to buy goods or services.

**PPO** — Pension Payment Order — the document that authorises a retired employee's pension.

**PT** — Professional Tax — a small state tax deducted from salaries.

## Q

**QR Code** — A square barcode that can be scanned by a phone camera to quickly open information (used on digital ID, invoices, etc.).

## R

**RE (Revised Estimate)** — The updated budget figure after part of the year has passed and actuals are known.

**Regularisation** — Correcting a missing or wrong attendance entry, with supervisor approval.

**Reverse Auction** — A live bidding event where suppliers compete by lowering their price in real time.

**Role** — A set of permissions that decides what a person is allowed to see and do in the system.

**RTGS** — Real Time Gross Settlement — a fast bank transfer used for larger amounts (usually above ₹2 lakh).

**RTI** — Right to Information — a citizen's request to see official records, to be answered within 30 days.

## S

**Sanction** — An official approval to spend a fixed amount of money for a stated purpose.

**SGST** — State Goods and Services Tax — the state government's share of GST on a transaction within the same state.

**SLA** — Service Level Agreement — the promised time within which a request or ticket should be resolved.

**SSO** — Single Sign-On — logging in once to reach all the parts you're allowed to use without signing in again.

## T

**TA-DA** — Travelling Allowance and Daily Allowance — money to cover official travel and trip costs.

**TDS** — Tax Deducted at Source — tax held back from a payment and sent to the tax department on the payee's behalf.

**Tenant** — Your office or organisation's own private workspace in the system, kept separate from other offices.

**Tender** — A formal, competitive process to choose a supplier for larger purchases, following procurement rules.

**3-way Match** — Checking that the purchase order, goods received note, and bill all agree before a payment is made.

**Trial Balance** — A check that total debits equal total credits across all accounts — used to verify books are correct.

## U

**UC (Utilisation Certificate)** — Proof that grant or scheme money was spent correctly and for the intended purpose.

**UTR** — Unique Transaction Reference — the number that identifies a bank transfer so you can trace it.

## V

**Voted** — Spending that needs Parliament's or the Assembly's approval through a formal vote.

**Charged** — Spending fixed by law that does not need a separate legislative vote.

**Voucher** — A document that records a payment or receipt before it is posted to accounts.

## W

**WBS** — Work Breakdown Structure — a project split into phases, stages, and tasks so progress can be tracked.

**Webhook** — An automatic notification sent to another system when something happens in CivitasOne.

## Numbers & Symbols

**3-way Match** — See "3-way Match" under T above.

## Can't Find a Term?

If you come across a word in CivitasOne that isn't in this glossary:
1. Hover over the **?** icon next to it on screen — a tooltip will usually explain it.
2. Ask the **AI Assistant** (tap "Ask" at the bottom right): "What does [term] mean?"
3. Contact your office admin or the Help Centre.
